This Privacy Policy explains how The Lobby ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use The Lobby mobile application ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Who We Are

The Lobby is an anonymous community chat platform built for Dubai. We are operated by Hashim Ashraf, based in Dubai, UAE. For any privacy-related enquiries, contact us at tlobbyapp@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to operate the App:

  • Nickname chosen by you at registration (no real name required)
  • Email address (for account authentication only)
  • Dubai area or neighbourhood (optional, chosen by you)
  • Interests selected during onboarding (optional)
  • Chat messages sent within rooms and private conversations
  • Device identifiers for push notification delivery
  • App usage data for performance and bug fixing

We do not collect your real name, phone number, profile photo, or any government-issued identification.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the anonymous chat service
  • To deliver push notifications for messages and activity
  • To match you with relevant community members and professionals (AI Concierge feature)
  • To improve app performance and fix technical issues
  • To comply with applicable UAE laws and regulations

4. Anonymity

The Lobby is built on the principle of anonymity. Your nickname is the only identity visible to other users. We will never display your real name, email address, or any personally identifying information to other users of the App.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure, which applies industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Firebase servers used by The Lobby are located in the United States. By using the App, you consent to your data being processed in accordance with Google Firebase's security standards.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Google Firebase for app infrastructure and data storage
  • With Anthropic's Claude API for AI Concierge introductions (anonymised data only)
  • When required by UAE law or a valid legal authority

7. Content Moderation

The Lobby operates in the UAE and complies with Federal Cybercrime Law No. 34 of 2021. All users must comply with our Terms of Service. We reserve the right to remove content and suspend accounts that violate UAE law or our community standards.

8. Your Rights

Under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent to data processing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at tlobbyapp@gmail.com. Account deletion is also available directly within the App settings.

9. Children's Privacy

The Lobby is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 years of age. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will immediately delete their account and associated data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
